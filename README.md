# 🏁 ObstacleOne – OCR Registration Platform

**ObstacleOne** is an open-source, volunteer-built registration and management platform for the global Obstacle Course Racing (OCR) community. Developed under the Elevinc nonprofit incubator, its goal is to provide athletes, federations, and event organizers with a unified, modern platform to manage events, memberships, performance data, and more.

---

## 🎯 Purpose

To empower the World Obstacle community with a modular, scalable platform that supports:
- Athlete registration and profile management
- Event signups with heat and category selection
- Federation memberships and revenue routing
- Race result tracking and performance metrics
- Admin dashboards for federations and organizers

This project is designed to simulate a full real-world product team environment, giving contributors the opportunity to gain hands-on experience in development, data, UI/UX, product, and DevOps.

---

## 🧱 Core Features (MVP Scope)

1. **Athlete Management**
   - Profile creation and editing
   - Unique athlete ID generation
   - Event history and ranking tracking

2. **Event Registration**
   - Register for events and categories (e.g., Elite, Age Group)
   - QR code confirmations and waiver support

3. **Federation Memberships**
   - Join national federations
   - Federation dashboards and revenue distribution (99% to NF, 1% to platform)

4. **Stats & Rankings**
   - Race history, placements, and finish times
   - Filters by age, gender, discipline, nationality

5. **Admin Interfaces**
   - Platform-wide views for global admins
   - Federation, event organizer, and check-in volunteer dashboards
